# Taste
- Prefers a node-graph / patch-bay style editor (TouchDesigner- and Max-for-Live-like) where generators and effects are modules wired together in series or in parallel, instead of a linear timeline of stacked clips. Confidence: 0.9
- Wants work stopped and the blocker explicitly documented when a required SDK, host machine, signing identity, or physical device is genuinely unavailable — rather than stubbed, faked, or worked around silently. Confidence: 0.8
- Prefers being handed exact absolute file paths and step-by-step instructions for any manual action left to them. Confidence: 0.55
- Wants long-running media processing (renders/exports) to show the real output live and looping on screen, not just a progress counter or status text. Confidence: 0.6
- Rejects "fixed — here's the rebuilt binary" handoffs that were only inferred from passing tests; the user expects a deliverable to be actually launched and exercised end-to-end before it is presented as working. Confidence: 0.55
- When several features are reported broken at once, wants an explicit, comprehensive test/measurement plan — sweeping every generator, effect, and preset rather than just the reported failure — written up and agreed before fixes are applied. Confidence: 0.75
